Privacy Policy

LabWise Health · Last updated 28 October 2026

LabWise Health reads laboratory report documents and explains the values in them. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, where it is stored, and who else can see it.

Who is responsible

Avetik Sukiasyan is the data controller for this app. For any question about your data, or to request its deletion, write to avo.sukiasyan90@gmail.com.

How your reports are read

The document you upload is parsed entirely on your device. The file is not sent anywhere to be analysed, and no artificial intelligence or machine learning service receives it. The values the app extracts are then saved to your account so your history and comparisons work across your devices.

Apple Health and Health Connect

LabWise Health requests read-only access to steps, walking, running and cycling distance, workouts and active energy. It never writes anything back. This data stays on your device, is not uploaded to any server, is never used for advertising, and is never sold or shared. Granting access is optional and the rest of the app works without it.

Who else receives your data

Google (Firebase). Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Storage host your account, your saved results and your uploaded files. Access is restricted by security rules so that only your signed-in account can read or write your data.

Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple handles the sign-in. If you choose Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address Apple generates.

Telegram. When an account is created for the first time, the app sends a notification to a private Telegram channel operated by the developer. That message contains your name, your email address, your account identifier, and basic device information such as model, operating system version, app version, language and time zone. It is used to monitor signups and is not used for advertising. If you would prefer this not to happen, contact the address above and it will be removed.

No analytics service, advertising network or data broker receives anything from this app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email avo.sukiasyan90@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and it will be erased within 30 days. Deleting the app from your phone removes the local copy but does not by itself delete what is stored in your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how it is used, or object to its use. Health information is treated as a special category of personal data under the GDPR, and the app relies on your consent as its legal basis for handling it. You can withdraw that consent at any time by requesting deletion.

Children

LabWise Health is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Not medical advice. LabWise Health does not diagnose. The explanations it shows are general information derived from the values printed on your document. Always discuss your results with a qualified clinician.
