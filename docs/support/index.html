Common questions

My report would not open, or no values appeared.
The app reads text from the document, so a scanned or photographed PDF cannot be used — it has to be a file where the text is selectable. If the text is there but few values were found, the laboratory's layout is probably not recognised yet. Email us the laboratory name and we will add support for it.
Why does it say my result needs attention?
A value is flagged when it falls outside the reference range printed on your own document. The app does not diagnose anything and cannot tell you what a result means for you personally. Discuss it with a clinician.
I uploaded the same report twice.
Uploading an identical file is refused, since it would tell you nothing new. Uploading a newer test of the same type replaces the old one and shows you which values improved or declined.
My activity calendar is empty.
Open the Plan tab and tap Connect to allow access to Apple Health or Health Connect. The app only reads steps, distance and workouts, and never writes anything back. If you are on a simulator rather than a real phone, there is no activity history to show.
I am not getting medicine reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed for LabWise Health in your device settings. On Android 12 and later, reminders also need permission to schedule exact alarms. A dose whose time has already passed when you add it will first remind you on the next scheduled day.
Can I sign in with both Google and Apple?
Yes. If both report the same email address, the second one is linked to your existing account. If you choose Hide My Email with Apple, Apple gives a different address that cannot be matched, so that creates a separate account.
How do I delete my account and data?
Email avo.sukiasyan90@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and everything will be erased within 30 days. Removing the app deletes the copy on your phone but not what is stored in your account.
